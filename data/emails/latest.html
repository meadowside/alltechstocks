  • Andy's Pick: Update: TSMC reportedly weighs 20% CoWoS price increase as Nvidia Blackwell and Rubin chips put pressure on packaging capacity (Tom's Hardware, 9 sources) TSM, NVDA
    So what: TSMC is reportedly preparing to raise prices for CoWoS, the advanced chip packaging service used to combine AI accelerators with high-bandwidth memory. Reported increases of up to 20% would land on chip designers that rely on the process to make their most in-demand processors. Customers such as Nvidia and AMD would face higher costs for the packaging step that sits between wafer fabrication and final assembly. The move would also strengthen TSMC's position in a segment where capacity remains tight and rivals are still scaling their own packaging output.
